# Rules Reference (GM Cheat Sheet)

Condensed from the Root RPG playbook pack and GM reference, plus the quickstart. Where the quickstart and core text differ, the core wording is used.

## Rolling

**2d6 + stat.** 10+ full hit. 7-9 partial hit (cost). 6- miss (GM makes a move).
Stats: **Charm, Cunning, Finesse, Luck, Might** (range -1 to +3 for starting characters).

## Basic moves (everyone has these)

| Move | Trigger | Stat | 10+ | 7-9 | 6- |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Persuade an NPC** | Promises or threats to an NPC | Charm | They see things your way (if you gave a strong motive or reasonable bribe) | They aren't sure. GM says what more you must do | GM move |
| **Figure Someone Out** | Studying a person | Charm | Hold 3 | Hold 1 | GM move |
| **Trick an NPC** | Deceive or manipulate an NPC to get what you want | Cunning | They take the bait | They take the bait, **or** choose one: hesitate (shaken), stumble (you get an opportunity), overreact (+1 forward vs them) | GM move |
| **Read a Tense Situation** | Taking in a dangerous or pressured scene | Cunning | Ask 3 | Ask 1 | GM move |
| **Attempt a Roguish Feat** | Trained roguish feat (state your goal) | Finesse | Achieve goal | Achieve goal, **and** mark exhaustion or one **risk** of the feat (GM's choice) | GM move |
| **Trust Fate** | Dangerous or tense thing with no other move | Luck | Scrape by or barrel through, **with a cost**, plus a fleeting opportunity | Scrape by or barrel through, **with a cost** | GM move |
| **Wreck Something** | Smash a thing it's possible to smash | Might | It's seriously broken (unusable until repaired) | As 10+, but collateral damage, attention, or a bad spot (GM's choice) | GM move |
| **Help or Interfere** | Aid or hinder another vagabond | none | Mark **exhaustion** to give **+1** (help) or **-2** (interfere) to their roll **after** they roll. Mark exhaustion **again** to **conceal** it **or** create an **opportunity/obstacle** | n/a | n/a |
| **Plead with a PC** | Ask another PC to go along with your plan | none | They **clear 1 exhaustion** if they agree. **Once per session**, each PC to each PC | n/a | n/a |

**Figure Someone Out questions** (spend 1 hold each, the target's player answers honestly):
- Is your character telling the truth?
- What is your character really feeling?
- What does your character intend to do?
- What does your character wish I'd do?
- How could I get your character to ___? ("You can't" is allowed.)

**Read a Tense Situation questions** (and take +1 when acting on the answers):
- What's my best way out / in / through?
- Who or what is the biggest threat?
- Who or what is most vulnerable to me?
- What should I be on the lookout for?
- Who is in control here?

**Notes:**
- Persuade and Trick **only work on NPCs**. Use Plead for PCs.
- If a player isn't trained in a roguish feat and tries it, it's **Trust Fate**.
- Trust Fate on **any hit still costs something**. Describe it (dropped item, exhaustion, noise).

## Roguish feats and their risks

| Feat | Covers | Risks (GM picks one on 7-9 if not exhaustion) |
|---|---|---|
| Acrobatics | Climbing, vaulting, jumping | Break something, detection, plunge into danger |
| Blindside | Backstab, sneak attack, suckerpunch | Draw unwanted attention, leave evidence, plunge into danger |
| Counterfeit | Copying, forgery, fakery | Leave evidence, take too long, weak result |
| Disable Device | Disarming traps and mechanisms | Break something, draw unwanted attention, expend resources |
| Hide | Disappear from view | Expend resources, leave evidence, take too long |
| Pick Lock | Locked doors, chests | Break something, detection, plunge into danger |
| Pickpocket | Subtly steal from a pocket | Leave evidence, take too long, weak result |
| Sleight of Hand | Palming, switching, ditching | Draw unwanted attention, leave evidence, weak result |
| Sneak | Get in or out unseen | Break something, draw unwanted attention, plunge into danger |

**Risk definitions:**
- **Break something:** something carried or in the world breaks (maybe mark wear)
- **Detection:** someone straight up notices
- **Draw unwanted attention:** hostile onlookers appear, or attention is called without detection
- **Expend resources:** mark depletion or exhaustion
- **Leave evidence:** leaves a trail for later investigation
- **Plunge into danger:** the chance to act drops them into something worse
- **Take too long:** time passes and the situation changes
- **Weak result:** a hard choice, or not quite everything they wanted

## Weapon moves

Everyone can use **Engage in Melee**, **Grapple**, and **Target** with a weapon of the right range. **Special** moves need **both** the weapon skill **and** a weapon tagged with it (Improvise Weapon and Confuse Senses only need usable materials).
Unarmed: **1 exhaustion** harm. Armed: **1 injury** harm.

| Move | Range | Stat | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engage in Melee** | Close / intimate | Might | Hit = **trade harm**. 10+ pick 3, 7-9 pick 1: inflict serious (+1) harm / suffer little (-1) harm / shift range one step / impress, dismay or frighten foe |
| **Grapple an Enemy** | Intimate | Might | On a hit both choose simultaneously, repeat until someone disengages, is unconscious, or dead. On 10+, you pick one first. Options: inflict injury / they mark exhaustion / mark exhaustion to inflict 2 injury / withdraw to close range |
| **Target Someone** | Far | Finesse | Hit = **inflict injury**. 10+ also strike again, or stay hidden |
| **Cleave** *(special)* | Close | Might | Mark exhaustion. Hit = inflict **3 wear** on armor/gear. 7-9: mark wear or end up in a bad spot |
| **Confuse Senses** *(special)* | Close / intimate | Finesse | Throw something. Hit = target off-balance/blinded, you get an opportunity. 10+ they need time to recover; 7-9 a few moments |
| **Disarm** *(special)* | Close | Finesse | Hit = foe marks **2 exhaustion** or loses weapon. 10+ = 3 exhaustion |
| **Harry a Group** *(special)* | Far | Cunning | Mark wear. 10+ both, 7-9 one: inflict **2 morale** / they're pinned or blocked |
| **Improvise Weapon** *(special)* | n/a | Cunning | Hit = you make a weapon (GM gives range tag and a benefit tag). 7-9 also a weakness tag |
| **Parry** *(special)* | Close | Finesse | Mark exhaustion. 10+ all 3, 7-9 pick 1: inflict morale or exhaustion / disarm (weapon in reach) / take no harm |
| **Quick Shot** *(special)* | Close | **Luck** | Hit = inflict injury. 10+ choose 2, 7-9 choose 1: no wear / no exhaustion / reposition (and maybe range) / keep target at bay |
| **Storm a Group** *(special)* | Melee vs group | Might | Mark exhaustion. Hit = trade harm. 10+ choose 2, 7-9 choose 1: 2 morale harm / 2 exhaustion on them / suffer less harm / they hurt each other (mark exhaustion again) |
| **Trick Shot** *(special)* | Any | Finesse | Mark wear. 10+ pick 3, 7-9 pick 2: hit hidden target / strike second target / cut or break something / distract for opportunity |
| **Vicious Strike** *(special)* | Intimate / close | Might | Mark exhaustion. Hit = serious (+1) harm and target **can't use armor wear** to block. 10+ clean; 7-9 they hit you back too |

**Range tags:** intimate, close, far.

## Harm tracks

| Track | Meaning | If it is full and you must mark | Clears when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Injury** | Physical health | **Dying** (passes out, will perish without aid) | Medical care or long rest |
| **Exhaustion** | Energy, will, stress | **Unconscious / at others' mercy** | About 1 per good night; all with rest and care; **all** when you fulfill your **Nature** |
| **Depletion** | Supplies and minor gear | Out of supplies (no other penalty) | Resupply, forage, pay |
| **Wear** | Per item | **Item breaks** | Repair (1 Value per box, needs a smith or Tinker) |
| **Morale** (NPCs only) | Will to fight | **Surrenders or flees** | n/a |

- A character can't voluntarily choose to mark a box they don't have.
- **Armor:** mark 1 wear instead of 1 injury (as many injury as makes sense at once), if armor would plausibly protect.
- Any general item (rope, torch, medical kit): **mark 1 depletion** to pull it from the pack. About **1 depletion = 1 Value = 1 injury healed by a skilled healer.**

## Equipment, Value and Burden

- **Value** pays for anything. 1 box of depletion = 1 Value.
- **Burden:** each significant item = 1 Load (some 0, some 2). **Burdened** at **4 + Might**; max is **twice** that. Burdened characters mark 1 exhaustion on long travel.
- Repair: 1 Value per box of wear, needs a crafter. A **broken** item must be replaced.
- Coin or a treasure: it's a "piece of equipment" with wear boxes equal to its Value. Coin can be spent one box at a time; a single object must be traded whole.
- Pre-made gear:

| Item | Wear | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chainmail | 3 | 3 | Tightly woven (repair 1 wear after fight); Weighty (+1 Load) |
| Leather Armor | 2 | 3 | Flexible (when grappling, mark exhaustion to ignore their first choice) |
| Plate Armor | 4 | 3 | Arrow-proof; Cumbersome (1 exhaustion to don); Weighty |
| Dagger | 1 | 5 | Intimate/close; Parry, Vicious Strike; Quick (exhaustion: Finesse in melee) |
| Mousefolk Short Sword | 2 | 5 | Close; Parry, Disarm; Mousefolk Steel (mark wear: melee with Cunning) |
| Shortbow | 4 | 6 | Close; Quick Shot; Short Limbs (mark wear: quick shot at far range) |

## Reputation (per faction, per PC)

- Factions tracked: **Denizens, Marquisate, Woodland Alliance** (plus Eyrie Dynasties off-screen).
- **Prestige** = positive; **Notoriety** = negative; **Reputation** = the circled number, -3 to +3.
- Boxes to climb: -2 to -1, -1 to 0, 0 to +1 cost **5 prestige** each; +1 to +2 costs **10**; +2 to +3 costs **15**.
- Boxes to drop: +3 to +2, +2 to +1, +1 to 0, 0 to -1 cost **3 notoriety** each; -1 to -2 costs **6**; -2 to -3 costs **9**.
- When several PCs' reputations are at stake, add them together (max +4, min -3).

| Move | When | Needs | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ask for a Favor** | Reasonable favor from a faction | Roll + Reputation | Hit: granted. 7-9: costs rep (clear 1 prestige or mark notoriety). Miss: refused, mark notoriety |
| **Meet Someone Important** | First meeting | Roll + Reputation with their faction | Hit: both know of each other's reputation. 7-9 pick one, 10+ both: (a) you've heard stories, ask one question, GM tells you a story; (b) they've heard something good about you, +1 forward when you play up the connection. Miss: you only know the basics; they've heard rumors of you. Expect complications |
| **Sway an NPC** | Appeal to an open or vulnerable NPC's belief in your name | +2 Reputation | Hit: you tell them what to believe; GM rewrites their drive. 7-9: clear 3 prestige. Miss: they take the wrong message |
| **Command Resources** | Demand significant resources | +3 Reputation | 10+: get them. 7-9: conditions. Miss: they tell you a hard way to get them |
| **Draw Attention** | Publicly make yourself a faction's enemy | -2 or lower | Roll as if positive. Hit: faction mobilizes. Pick 1 (7-9) or 2 (10+): military chases / capable NPC agent chases / faction is rattled (mark 2 notoriety on them) |
| **Make a Pointed Threat** | Threaten using your fearsome name | -3 | Roll as +3. 10+: they surrender, retreat, or charge. 7-9: need a demonstration first |

(Quickstart simplified list: Endorse or Vilify a Leader, Intimidate Enemy Troops, Lead Troops in Battle, and the faction-specific Prestige moves exist in the full core book.)

## Travel moves (used on the journey to Bertram's Cove or later)

**Travel Through the Forest** (band picks how, one rolls for all):
- Slowly, foraging: everyone clears 2 depletion; band marks 1 exhaustion each; **-1**
- Carefully: band marks 1 depletion or exhaustion each; **+1**
- Quickly: everyone marks exhaustion **and** depletion; **+2**
- Hit: reach the far clearing, spot a site of interest. 10+ safe. 7-9 something follows (it catches up, or everyone marks exhaustion to lose it). Miss: a forest danger you must deal with first.

**Travel Along the Path:**
- Relaxed: everyone clears 3 exhaustion; band marks 2 depletion; **-1**
- Average: everyone clears 2 exhaustion; band marks 1 depletion; **+0**
- Safely and quickly: band marks 1 depletion; **+1**
- Urgently: everyone marks exhaustion; **+2**
- Hit: arrive in good time. 10+ quick and clean. 7-9 something noteworthy on the path. Miss: caught in a dangerous situation before arriving.

Travel by ship to the Cove takes **a day and a night** (see adventure guide). The overland route takes a fortnight or more.

## Connections (the little rules between PCs)

| Connection | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Friend** | When you help them, mark **2 exhaustion** to give **+2** instead of 1 exhaustion for +1 |
| **Partner** | Each marks **2 prestige** with a helped faction and **2 notoriety** with a harmed faction when taken. When **spotted together**, prestige/notoriety gains are **doubled** |
| **Professional** | When you share info after Read a Tense Situation, **both** get the +1. When helping them with a roguish feat, you get help options as if you spent 2 exhaustion but only spend 1 |
| **Family** | When you help them fulfill their **Nature**, you **both clear exhaustion** |
| **Protector** | When they're in reach, mark exhaustion to **take a blow meant for them**; then **+1 ongoing to weapon moves** for the scene |
| **Watcher** | When you **figure them out**, you hold 1 **even on a miss**. When you plead, they can clear **2 exhaustion** instead of 1 |

## Advancement

- Each PC has **2 drives**. When you meet a drive's condition, the GM agrees, and you **advance once per drive per session** (max 2 advances per session).
- Choose one: **+1 stat** (max +2), **new move from own playbook** (max 5), **new move from another playbook** (max 2), **two new weapon skills** (max 7), **two new roguish feats** (max 6), **+1 box on a harm track** (max 6), **up to two new connections** (max 6).
- Players may **change** one playbook element (drive, nature, connection) at the end of a session. Optional.
- **Extra options on the reference sheet (full core book, page 170):** basic move **mastery** (max 2) and weapon skill **mastery** (up to 2, max 4). The book has the details; they are not needed in session one.

## Special rules from Bertram's Cove

- **Deepwater Recovery** (searching the Wavebreaker): say what and where you search. Ask: *Are you a good swimmer? Is the water shallow? Are you free of threats nearby?* Each **yes** gives **+1** to the roll. **10+** you find it. **7-9** you find something else useful. **Miss:** you're snared; mark **exhaustion equal to (9 minus your roll total)**. If you can't mark enough exhaustion, you drown. Daisy's **untested diving rig** gives **+3 ongoing** to this move and reduces a miss to **1 exhaustion**.
- **Swinging Ship to Ship:** daring acrobatics; say where you're going and **roll + Finesse**. Hit: you make it and **mark exhaustion**. 10+: you gain the upper hand, spot a weakness, or expose a flaw. 7-9: you're in a tight spot. Miss: you land exactly where you didn't want to be.
