## Session flow (about 2h50)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Intros, safety line, connections |
| 0:20 | **Dawn at the docks:** Daisy, Montague, posters |
| 0:30 | **Fire on the water:** burning supply ship, everyone acts |
| 0:55 | **Free investigation:** A Daisy/docks, B fort, C town/rebels |
| 1:40 | **Roundup** at dusk (moral beat) |
| 1:55 | **The cart:** Aiden, soldiers, ambush |
| 2:40 | Aftermath, advances, cliffhanger |

## Three clue rule
Three separate sources must say **"hospital, tonight"** before Midnight: **Daisy** (heavy blanket crates, acrid smell) / **Dubois** (powder bought in three shops; crates behind the Fishmonger's) / **Tilda (added)** (the bomb is for the hospital) / **Aiden** (boasting in the tavern) / a roundup prisoner.
**Safety valves:** Daisy tugs a sleeve / Tilda begs the kindest PC / a smell and a crash from a cart / a note from Dubois's agent.

## Fast NPC stats (Injury / Exhaustion / Wear / Morale)
Default denizen **1/1/1/1**. Brute **3/2/3/2**. Leader **1/2/1/3**. Lieutenant **2/2/3/3**. Small mob **3 of each, x2 harm**. Marquisate squad hits for **3 injury**; Alliance squad **2 injury**. **Morale breaks most fights.**

## Moves to call for

| Do this | Move (roll) |
|---|---|
| Talk | Persuade (Charm), Figure Out (Charm) |
| Lie or distract | Trick (Cunning) |
| Assess danger | Read a Tense Situation (Cunning) |
| Sneak, climb, lock, pocket | Roguish Feat (Finesse) |
| Risky, no other move | Trust Fate (Luck) |
| Smash | Wreck Something (Might) |
| Help a friend | Mark exhaustion: +1 (+2 with Friend) |
| Swing between ships | **Swinging Ship to Ship** (Finesse) |

**10+** win. **7-9** win with a cost. **6-** you make a move. **You never roll.**

## When stuck
Carrot or stick / show a faction's fangs / threaten their reputation / ask "What do you notice?" / **escalate**.

## Escalation menu
Wheelbarrow barrel cracks / soldiers drag neighbors out / jaunty red hat flashes down an alley / fort password changes **(added)** / bounty hunter offers a lead **(added)** / sick sailor begs for a letter delivered **(added)**.

## Climax complications (on a 7-9 or a miss)
Barrel leaks / torch falls / soldier shouts, everyone freezes / nurse with a lantern appears / **Montague** arrives and orders stand down / **Hookpaw** steps from the dark / fire begins, evacuate and fight it.

## Ways to solve it
**Aiden:** Persuade, Trick, Figure Out, Orator, Fast Friends, Plead. **Bomb:** Disable Device, Toolbox, Jury Rig, Pick Lock, Wreck, dump in the harbor. **Soldiers:** Persuade, Trick, Carry a Big Stick, Confuse Senses. **Rebels:** Read a Tense Situation, Harry a Group, Persuade Hookpaw (strong motive only).

## Prize and ending
**Prize:** knowledge (Saffron leads the Alliance; Sparrowhawk is Kell) plus prestige, about **6-Value** purse **(added)**, favors, Aiden as a friend. Bounty (12-Value + pass) is for **catching Sparrowhawk**, not for the bomb.
**Cliffhangers:** Saffron knows you / Dubois asks for Michel / Daisy needs divers / Declan's letter / Montague offers the bounty.
