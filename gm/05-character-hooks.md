# Character Hooks and Spotlights

**GM only.** The quickstart's pregenerated characters each had bonds to Bertram's Cove. Your players use core playbooks instead, so these are **our adapted equivalents (added)**. Each PC has an optional "tie" on their sheet (spoiler-free). Here is how to use it, plus how each can hit their drives in this adventure.

## Connections (suggested ring)

| Player | Connection 1 | Connection 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Marline (Adventurer) | Partner: Katie | Friend: Ben |
| Katie (Arbiter) | Partner: Marline | Protector: Noah |
| Michelle (Harrier) | Professional: Nick | Friend: Noah |
| Ben (Ranger) | Watcher: Nick | Protector: Marline |
| Nick (Thief) | Professional: Michelle | Friend: Ben |
| Noah (Tinker) | Professional: Michelle | Family: Katie |

Partner connections give **2 prestige** (faction helped) and **2 notoriety** (faction harmed). Suggest Marline and Katie say they once helped the **Denizens** of a lakeside clearing against **Eyrie** raiders but lost. Write "Eyrie" on a blank reputation line if needed.

## Per-player hooks

### Marline, Adventurer
- **Tie:** Grew up in Oar's Rest (across the lake). **Saffron Goodacre** inspired her with stories of adventurers toppling tyrants.
- **Use it:** Saffron greets her **warmly** and remembers the girl on the dock. That makes it **hard** to believe what Saffron is planning, and powerful when she finds out. Also **Aiden Birch's older sister Rose** was Marline's childhood best friend (optional addition from the module's bonds): it gives her **a way into Aiden's heart**: "Rose never stops talking about you."
- **Spotlight beats:** Montague at the dock (Persuade), Saffron's study, talking Aiden down (Orator, Fast Friends).
- **Drives:** *Ambition* (rep up with a faction: easy with the burning ship), *Justice* (justice for someone wronged by the powerful: the sympathizers in the roundup, or Aiden if he's being used).

### Michelle, Harrier
- **Tie:** A sibling in Bertram's Cove: **Bill "Hookpaw" Hayward**, now a hard-fisted Alliance second-in-command. (From the module's Sherwin the Harrier.)
- **Use it:** Hookpaw recognizes her **immediately** at some point. He is **proud and resentful**. In the climax he is **the one waiting in the dark**. She might be the **only one** who can make him hesitate. Big drama: a squirrel against her own brother.
- **Spotlight beats:** burning ship (**Swinging Ship to Ship**), tailing Aiden (**Parkour**, **Smuggler's Path**), the confrontation with Hookpaw.
- **Drives:** *Wanderlust* (finished a journey: she can advance right at the start; use it as a reward and a teachable moment), *Crime* (an illicit caper against impressive odds: stopping or stealing the cart?).

### Ben, Ranger
- **Tie:** Knows the **rocky, old-growth woods** east of the Cove. (Adapted: the module's secret Alliance base is hidden in such terrain.)
- **Use it:** If he **asks the right question** (Talon on the Pulse isn't his; use **Read a Tense Situation**), the GM can tell him that **fishing boats patrol the shore** and **there's a hidden camp** up the granite canyon. That seeds **Michel's prison** for session two. Also: spotting **hidden rebels** in the alleys near the hospital is a classic Ranger moment.
- **Spotlight beats:** tracking Aiden's cart, hiding in the alley, **Poisons and Antidotes** to help the sick sailors or knock out a guard.
- **Drives:** *Discovery* (a new wonder or ruin in the forests: hint at the hidden Alliance base or the wreck), *Freedom* (free a group from oppression: the roundup prisoners, or Michel later).

### Noah, Tinker
- **Tie:** Has traded letters with **Daisy Delaware**, the dockmaster-tinkerer. (Adapted: Daisy invents gadgets.)
- **Use it:** Daisy greets him like an old pen pal. She's **thrilled** to show him her **untested diving rig** and ask what he thinks. It makes him the **natural partner** for the **Wavebreaker** thread. In the climax, he is **the bomb person**: **Toolbox**, **Jury Rig**, **Disable Device**.
- **Spotlight beats:** Daisy's workshop, examining the leaking barrel, **Toolbox** conditions ("quiet, a flat surface, and no flames").
- **Drives:** *Ambition* (rep up), *Greed* (a serious payday or treasure: the Wavebreaker gold, or the Marquisate bounty).

### Katie, Arbiter
- **Tie:** Previously hired by **Commandant Astrid Montague** as an escort. (Added.)
- **Use it:** Montague **remembers** and tries to **hire her** again. A loyalty trap: Montague will ask for help hunting rebels. Katie must decide where she stands. (If she takes the job, **Marquisate prestige**; but the Alliance sees her as an enemy.)
- **Spotlight beats:** **Guardian** during the burning ship rescue, **Carry a Big Stick** during the roundup, standing between soldiers and townsfolk, then between rebels and the hospital.
- **Drives:** *Justice* (the roundup victims), *Principles* (embodying her principles at great cost: refusing to stand with either side at the hospital).

### Nick, Thief
- **Tie:** Heard a **rumor of the Wavebreaker's payroll chest**. (Added.)
- **Use it:** Daisy's pitch for the dive appeals to him. He's also the best option for **sneaking into the fort**, the **net loft**, or the **Alliance base** later. **Nose for Gold** points to the most valuable thing: **Saffron's notebook**, **Dubois's tattoos** (cipher keys), or **the bounty**.
- **Spotlight beats:** pickpocketing a rebel's note, sneaking into the net loft, picking the lock of the **hospital storeroom** or the crate itself.
- **Drives:** *Greed* (a serious payday: the bounty or the chest), *Thrills* (escape certain death or incarceration: caught at the fort or in the explosion).

## Spreading the spotlight

Track it informally on a sheet: for each scene, a tick next to whoever had a **big moment**. After Scene 2, if someone has zero ticks, give them the next **spotlight** question: "Nick, what is your character doing while everyone else is at the docks?"

## Connections you can trigger

- **Partner (Marline and Katie):** if they're seen together by a faction, their prestige and notoriety are **doubled**. Use it to raise stakes ("The soldiers saw you both. Double notoriety if you do that...").
- **Protector (Ben and Katie):** when someone takes a blow for their ally, it's a **great moment** and gives **+1 ongoing to weapon moves** for the scene. Remind them.
- **Professional (Michelle, Nick, Noah):** reading a tense situation as a team gets **both +1**.
- **Friend (Marline-Ben, Michelle-Noah, Nick-Ben):** **help for +2** by marking 2 exhaustion; remind them in a crunch.
- **Family (Noah-Katie):** when one fulfills their **Nature**, the other helps and **both clear exhaustion**.
- **Watcher (Ben on Nick):** Ben holds **1** even on a miss when he figures Nick out.
