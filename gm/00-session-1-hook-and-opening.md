# Session 1: Hook, Opening, Prize and What Comes Next

**GM only. Spoilers.** One-page reference. Details live in `04-adventure-guide.md`. Anything marked **(added)** is ours, not the module's.

---

## 1. The hook in one line

**A day and a night after leaving the far shore, six vagabonds step off the *Twice Sunk* into a town that is quietly at war with itself, and before the morning is over, a ship burns in the harbor and everyone on the dock has to act.**

## 2. Where they start

- **Place:** the inspection dock at **Bertram's Cove**, at dawn, aboard the ***Twice Sunk***.
- **Who is there:** **Daisy Delaware** (dockmaster, boards to collect harbor tax and gossip) and **Commandant Astrid Montague** (on the pier chewing out soldiers). **Captain Sparrowhawk WANTED** posters on every post.
- **Why the six are together:** one crossing, one ship, and the connections they set up in character creation. Each player also gives a reason for coming to the Cove (a job, a rumor, or a person).

### Read-aloud opening (adapt freely)

> The *Twice Sunk* creeps toward Bertram's Cove, a smudge of smoke and masts against a pink dawn. The air is salt and fish, tar and breakfast fires. Out on the water a big Marquisate brig sits scarred and silent under repair. Beyond it the Grand Lake stretches until you can't see land.
>
> Your ship bumps the inspection dock. On every post, every wall, the same face stares back: red eyes, scars, too many teeth. **"CAPTAIN SPARROWHAWK. WANTED DEAD OR ALIVE."**
>
> A mouse in a dockmaster's coat is already striding up your plank, one paw out for the harbor tax and the other somehow already holding a notebook. Past her, a gleaming-armored cat is dressing down a squad of soldiers.
>
> *Daisy Delaware looks you over and smiles.* "Welcome to the Cove. Names, cargo, and the real reason you're here. I'll know if you lie."

**Then ask each player one question:**
- "What does your character notice first?"
- "Who here do you recognize?" (use their tie from `05-character-hooks.md`)
- "What does your character want in the Cove?"

Finish with: **"What do you do?"**

## 3. The first crisis (about 10 minutes in)

While they are still on the dock, a **Marquisate supply ship loaded with medical supplies** pushes off and **bursts into flames**. Sailors leap overboard. With nobody at the wheel, a gust catches the sails and the **burning ship drifts toward the moored fishing fleet**. Bells ring. Montague bellows for help.

### Why it brings them together
- They are all already on the dock; there's no time to argue.
- Every playbook has something useful to do (see the table in `04-adventure-guide.md`, Scene 2).
- It teaches the dice with real stakes.
- It earns **prestige**, and Montague and Daisy notice the newcomers.
- It plants the first clue: a sailor mutters about "a little keg that shouldn't have been aboard", or someone finds the **burnt remains of an incendiary device**. The Alliance is escalating, and the medical supplies were headed to the **hospital**.

## 4. The hook that keeps them moving

After the fire, three pulls work at once:

| Pull | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Carrot** | Montague wants actionable intel and will pay. Dubois wants his missing agent back and pays in coin, favors or information. Daisy trades information and dangles a diving rig and a share of a treasure. |
| **Stick** | The Marquisate is suspicious of strangers and starts a **dusk roundup** of suspected rebel sympathizers. Being a vagabond in the wrong place is dangerous. |
| **Personal** | Each player's tie: Saffron's stories (Marline), the brother Hookpaw (Michelle), the hidden woods (Ben), Daisy's letters (Noah), Montague's old contract (Katie), the sunk payroll chest (Nick). |

By **dusk**, at least **three separate sources** should have told them: *something is going to happen at the hospital tonight.* That is the shared crisis. See the **three clue rule** and **safety valves** in the adventure guide.

## 5. The shared goal for the session

**Stop whatever is coming to the hospital tonight, and protect the people in it.** Everything else is optional and can wait for session two.

## 6. What is the prize?

The "prize" is a mix of a story reward, tangible rewards, and a new problem. Hand out the ones that fit what the players did.

### The real prize: knowledge and leverage
By the end, the party knows **Saffron Goodacre**, the beloved treasurer, is the **secret leader of the Alliance**, and that **Captain Sparrowhawk is a costume**. They will be among the only people in the Cove who know both. That information is worth more than gold to Montague, Dubois, the Alliance and the townsfolk.

### Tangible rewards (pick what fits)
| Reward | When to give it |
|---|---|
| **Prestige** with the Denizens (1 to 2), plus Marquisate, Alliance or both depending on how they won | They saved the hospital |
| **A grateful ally:** Aiden Birch, if he lived and they treated him well. Optional fate: he joins the band, turns himself in, or flees | They talked Aiden down |
| **A purse from the Marquisate (added):** about **6-Value** shared among the party, plus a **favor** from Montague | Montague learns they stopped an attack |
| **Dubois's debt (added):** one **favor** or one **item of information** | They share clues about Michel or the rebels |
| **Daisy's goodwill:** access to the **untested diving rig** | They helped her or her workshop |
| **Resupply:** clear depletion at the hospital or inn | Always, as a short rest |
| **Advancement:** each PC checks their drives and advances if met | End of session |

### The Sparrowhawk bounty (do **not** give for the bomb)
The big prize in town (**a full resupply, 12-Value in gold and a one-time Marquisate passage token**) is for **capturing or killing Sparrowhawk**. The PCs now know he is **Kell**, a frightened fisherman. That makes it a moral choice and a session two driver.

## 7. Endings and the next session

Don't pick the ending. Let the choices land, then offer **one or two** of these leads. Each outcome has a built-in "now what?".

| How session 1 ends | The cliffhanger | Pulls into session 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **They stop the bomb and save Aiden** | **"Saffron knows who you are."** She is warm, thanks them, and asks for a private word. Or Hookpaw finds out who ruined the plan. | **Decision:** tell Montague (Marquisate prestige, Saffron arrested, Alliance furious), keep the secret, join Saffron, or confront her |
| **They stop the bomb but Aiden is caught** | **Montague has Aiden.** He'll talk under pressure, and the PCs know what he'll reveal. | Free him, or let events unfold. Saffron wants him silenced |
| **The bomb goes off in the street, not the hospital** | **Martial law.** Montague locks down the Cove. Everyone is a suspect. | Escape the cordon, hunt the culprits, or clear their names |
| **They fail** | **Fire in the Cove.** Aiden is dead, patrols everywhere, **fishing banned**. | They are the only ones who know the truth. Revenge or justice |

### Always-available hooks (pick one to close the session)
1. **Dubois corners them:** *"My agent Michel Lambert went missing two nights ago. You seem useful. Find him."* The prisoner is held at the secret Alliance base. If nobody acts, **three suspects are killed, Michel among them**.
2. **Daisy shows up at dawn:** *"I know where the Wavebreaker went down. I need strong backs, a boat, and someone who can dive."* A **military payroll chest** and **classified documents**. Both sides offer a 20% bounty.
3. **Declan Sheehan arrives with a letter:** *"I think someone is about to get Kell killed."* If Kell isn't saved, he is captured and **hanged**.
4. **Saffron invites them to dinner:** she wants to know which side they're on.
5. **Montague offers the bounty:** *"Bring me Sparrowhawk, and you'll never need to work again."*

### Quick closing line
> "The fire in the harbor is out, the smoke has cleared, and by morning the whole Cove will know six strangers were there. What you do with what you know about tonight is up to you."
