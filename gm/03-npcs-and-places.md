# NPCs and Stat Blocks (Bertram's Cove)

**GM only. Contains spoilers.** Source: *Bertram's Cove* quickstart. Boxes are listed as **Injury / Exhaustion / Wear / Morale** and were counted from the printed squares in the PDF text. If a fight drags, trim the numbers. Talkers (Saffron, Dubois, Daisy) are not built to fight.

## How NPC combat works (quick)

- An NPC's weapon has a **range** and **harm** (standard blade = 1 injury, axe or large blade = 2 injury, whip = 1 injury + 1 exhaustion, hammer = 1 injury + 1 wear).
- When a PC hits with **Engage in Melee** you trade harm: NPC takes harm on a track you choose from the fiction (injury for a blade, exhaustion for a shove, wear on armor, **morale** when you frighten them).
- When any NPC track is full and they must mark again: injury = down/dying; exhaustion = collapses; wear = armor or weapon breaks; morale = **surrenders or flees**.
- **Morale is your friend.** Most fights should end in surrender or flight, not death.

### Default NPC (any denizen)
1 injury, 1 exhaustion, 1 wear, 1 morale. Standard blade (1 injury).

### Pre-made track templates
| Type | Injury / Exhaustion / Wear / Morale |
|---|---|
| Standard denizen | 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 |
| Brute or bruiser | 3 / 2 / 3 / 2 |
| Leader (not a fighter) | 1 / 2 / 1 / 3 |
| Lieutenant | 2 / 2 / 3 / 3 |
| A bear | 5 / 5 / 2 / 4 |

### Mobs (core rules)
| Group size | Mob | Tracks | Harm dealt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-10 | Small | 3 of each | x2 |
| 10-20 | Medium | 5 of each | x3 |
| 20+ | Large | 7 of each | x4 |

## Principal NPCs

### Astrid Montague (cat, Marquisate Commandant)
- **Description:** Gleaming armor, wanted posters all around her, dressing down troops on the pier.
- **Truth:** Never wanted to be a politician. A temporary posting turned permanent. Likes the townsfolk. Failure here ends her career.
- **Drive:** To end the rebellion.
- **Tracks:** Injury 8 / Exhaustion 8 / Wear 8 / Morale 4
- **Equipment:** High-quality armor set, greatsword (2 injury, +1 if she swings hard), spyglass.
- **Moves:** Call in reinforcements. Make a strong tactical decision. Strike with overwhelming violence.
- **How to play her:** Direct, weary, honorable. She's desperate for **actionable intel**. She suspects the armory or docks are the target. She hasn't considered the hospital.

### Saffron Goodacre (mouse, secret Alliance leader)
- **Description:** Moves "with the practiced grace of a diplomat". Kind voice. Robes. Notebook. Bottle of good wine.
- **Truth:** Calculating, willing to sacrifice ethics for victory. Planned the hospital bomb. Plans a **secondary attack on responding soldiers.**
- **Drive:** To drive the Marquisate from Bertram's Cove.
- **Tracks:** Injury 4 / Exhaustion 4 / Wear 6 / Morale **8** (a talker, not a fighter)
- **Equipment:** Notebook, flowing robes, bottle of good wine.
- **Moves:** Act with ruthless cunning. Lie with practiced ease. Manipulate others to insurrection.
- **How to play her:** Warm, patient, grandmotherly. She will gladly help the PCs *if* they help the cause. She is difficult to out. Her **inner circle doubts** the bomb.

### Aiden Birch (young mouse, Alliance volunteer)
- **Description:** Adolescent mouse, wheelbarrow, torch, trembling pipe-paw, a dull sword, a book of stories.
- **Truth:** Left his village (Oar's Rest, across the lake) to prove he's braver than his sister **Rose** (a Marquisate hero with medals). Volunteered because no one else would deliver the bomb. No training to arm it.
- **Drive:** To prove that he's brave.
- **Tracks:** Injury 2 / Exhaustion 2 / Wear 2 / Morale 4
- **Equipment:** Dull sword, a book of stories.
- **Moves:** Act with reckless abandon. Mask feelings with bravado. Attack a problem with brute force.
- **How to play him:** Over-confident, scared underneath. He can be **talked down**, shamed, inspired, or tricked. He is the **key emotional beat** of the session. Whether Aiden lives is entirely up to the players.

### Bill "Hookpaw" Hayward (squirrel, Alliance second-in-command)
- **Description:** Thick-necked, knuckledusters, "enormous dolly tow hook", sack of bread and cheese.
- **Truth:** Zealous. Interrogating suspects for the missing journal. Will kill the three likeliest suspects (including Michel) if no answers arrive.
- **Drive:** To get the Marquisate spy to confess.
- **Tracks:** Injury 8 / Exhaustion 8 / Wear 2 / Morale 4
- **Weapon:** Hook or knuckledusters, 2 injury.
- **Moves:** Hook someone's paw. Intimidate into submission. Undertake extreme action out of loyalty.

### Kell Sweetgrass / "Captain Sparrowhawk" (mouse fisherman)
- **Description:** Unremarkable, smiling mouse. The poster looks nothing like him.
- **Truth:** Reluctantly plays Sparrowhawk. Hidden at the Alliance base. Terrified, homesick. Letters from Declan hidden in an old tree knot.
- **Drive:** To get safely home to Declan.
- **Tracks:** Injury 4 / Exhaustion 4 / Wear 2 / Morale 4
- **Equipment:** Sparrowhawk costume (massive faux cutlass), letter from Declan, a bit of cheese in cloth.
- **Moves:** Embody Captain Sparrowhawk. Make someone feel at home. Blend in by being himself.
- **Bounty on his head:** a **full resupply, 12-Value in gold, and a one-time Marquisate passage token.**

### Declan Sheehan (burly mouse, fisherman)
- **Description:** Gentle, hardworking. Lives with Kell's parents.
- **Drive:** To rescue Kell from Sparrowhawk.
- **Tracks:** Injury 4 / Exhaustion 8 / Wear 2 / Morale 4
- **Moves:** Plead with sincerity. Inspire with loving words. Provide steadfast support.
- **How to play him:** He doesn't care who wins the war. He'll help the PCs if it gets Kell home.

### Daisy Delaware (mouse, dockmaster and tinkerer)
- **Description:** Walks up the plank to collect harbor taxes and gossip. Neutral. Windowless workshop shed near the pier.
- **Drive:** To seize every opportunity the war presents.
- **Tracks:** Injury 2 / Exhaustion 4 / Wear 4 / Morale 8
- **Equipment:** Toolbag, marking chalk, small plain dagger.
- **Moves:** Create a new gadget. Deploy the right tool for the job. Find anything in a mess.
- **Special:** **Untested diving rig** (+3 ongoing to Deepwater Recovery; miss costs only 1 exhaustion). She offers a **50/50 split** of the Wavebreaker gold.
- **How to play her:** The party's friendly information desk. She'll introduce them to either side. Neutral, but sells secrets.

### Maître Espion Dubois (cat, Marquisate spymaster)
- **Description:** Gregarious, quick to laugh. Officially a "hospitality and information logistics officer". Cipher key tattoos, soft shoes, lockpicks.
- **Drive:** To secure the return of Michel Lambert.
- **Tracks:** Injury 4 / Exhaustion 4 / Wear 8 / Morale 8
- **Moves:** Learn a secret through casual conversation. Disappear into the shadows. Disarm with gentle joviality.
- **How to play him:** Charming host. He'll **pay in coin, information or favors** to get Michel back. He's the one who orders the night raids on suspected sympathizers.

### Michel Lambert (mouse, deep-cover agent, alias "Tomasy Goodtail")
- **Description:** Infiltration specialist, currently a prisoner in the Alliance's secret base.
- **Tracks:** Injury 4 / Exhaustion 6 / Wear 2 / Morale 8 *(he has no gear while imprisoned)*
- **Gear (when free):** Cane with hidden sword, boot knife, bottle of disappearing ink.
- **Moves:** Lie with practiced ease. Withstand interrogation. Hide objects in plain sight.
- **Secrets he holds:** location of the secret base, guard shifts, fortifications, and **hidden proof that Saffron is the Alliance leader**.

## Groups

### Marquisate squad (8 soldiers, small mob)
- **Tracks:** Injury 8 / Exhaustion 8 / Wear 8 / Morale 4 *(squad-level)*
- **Harm inflicted:** **3 injury**
- **Notes:** Disciplined, professional. About **70 soldiers and staff** in the fort. Standard arms and armor. For single soldiers: use NPC rules with **at least 3 wear** (armor) and a weapon doing **2 injury**.
- **Drive:** To quell the rebellion.

### Alliance rebel squad (8 rebels, small mob)
- **Tracks:** Injury 6 / Exhaustion 6 / Wear 6 / Morale 8 *(squad-level)*
- **Harm inflicted:** **2 injury**
- **Notes:** About **50 active** local fighters. Superb on Grand Lake. Fight for their homes, so they are loyal. Scavenged armor and makeshift deadly weapons. For single rebels: **at least 2 wear**, weapon **2 injury**.
- **Drive:** To free Bertram's Cove from Marquisate rule.

### Simplified for your first game
If the squad numbers feel like too much bookkeeping, use the core **small mob**: **3 boxes of each track**, deals **x2 harm**. For a big fight, one mob per side is plenty.
