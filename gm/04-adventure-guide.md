# Adventure Guide: "The Night the Cove Nearly Burned"

**GM only. Spoilers throughout.**
Working title (ours). Built on the *Bertram's Cove* quickstart clearing. Anything marked **(added)** is our invention, not from the module. Rename, cut, or replace it freely.

**What is ours vs. the module's:** the module gives the situation, NPCs, places, conflicts, "how it develops" outcomes and a list of escalations. It does **not** give a scene-by-scene plan. The session flow, the clock, the burning-ship opener, the three leads, Tilda Fenn, Dubois's powder tip, the cart showdown, and the "three clue rule" are **our additions** to suit a first-time GM and six new players.

Designed for **6 new players, one 2.5 to 3 hour session**, with room to continue.

---

## 1. The adventure on one page

**The problem.** The Marquisate occupies Bertram's Cove. Rebel pirates of the **Woodland Alliance** raid its supply ships under a legendary captain, **Captain Sparrowhawk**. The Marquisate commandant **Astrid Montague** wants him dead or alive.

**The secret.**
1. Sparrowhawk is a **costume**. **Kell Sweetgrass**, a cheerful fisherman, plays him. The grim poster looks nothing like him.
2. The real Alliance leader is the kindly town treasurer **Saffron Goodacre**.
3. Saffron plans to **bomb the Marquisate-funded hospital** tonight, where a company of sailors is quarantined with the **Cuttlefish Cough**, and then **ambush the soldiers who rush to help**. Townsfolk use that hospital too.
4. Her own inner circle is uneasy. The only volunteer to deliver the bomb is **Aiden Birch**, a reckless young mouse who doesn't know how to arm it. Left alone, a pipe ember **sets it off early**, killing Aiden, burning part of the Cove, and leading Montague to declare **martial law and ban fishing on the lake**.

**What the party does.** They arrive in the morning. Over the day they find out (from several directions) that something terrible is coming tonight. At night they must stop it, and decide what they do with what they know.

**Why it works for beginners:** a **ticking clock**, a **clear villainous plan**, a sympathetic kid at the center, **many ways to win** (talk, sneak, fight, build, steal), and a **moral choice** that sets up session two.

## 2. Before the session (prep checklist)

- [ ] Print the six player sheets in `players/` plus `players/00-how-to-play.md` and `players/01-woodland-and-the-cove.md`
- [ ] Have players **pick or confirm** their quick builds (see `players/`). Allow **45 minutes** if building at the table, or have them done beforehand.
- [ ] Print or hand-draw the **wanted poster** ("CAPTAIN SPARROWHAWK, WANTED DEAD OR ALIVE", with a ridiculously grim face). Show it at the table. It's a great prop.
- [ ] Print `gm/01-gm-quick-start.md` (and keep it next to you) and the move tables in `gm/02-rules-reference.md`
- [ ] Draw a **clock** on a sheet: four boxes labelled **Dusk, Nightfall, Midnight, Boom** (see section 4)
- [ ] Index cards: Daisy, Montague, Dubois, Saffron, Aiden, Tilda **(added)**, with a one-line voice for each
- [ ] Drinks, snacks, d6 x2 per player (twelve dice if you can), pencils, paper for notes
- [ ] Re-read section 3 (the flow) once. Skim sections 5-8 when you need them.

### The first five minutes at the table
- Say: **"This is a game about vagabonds who get into trouble and change the story."** Explain the 10+/7-9/6- results and that **you never roll**.
- Safety line (see GM quick start): dark moments exist, speak up any time.
- Go around: each player says name, species, one look detail, and **why they came to Bertram's Cove**.
- Do connections quickly using the suggestions in `players/`.

## 3. The session flow (about 2 hours 50 minutes)

| Time | Beat | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:20 | **Welcome, character intros, connections** | Everyone feels ready. Safety line |
| 0:20-0:30 | **Scene 1: Dawn at the docks** | Meet Daisy and Montague. See the posters. Learn the situation |
| 0:30-0:55 | **Scene 2: Fire on the water** | First action. Teaches rolls. Everyone helps. Earn reputation |
| 0:55-1:40 | **Act 2: Free investigation** (three leads) | Players pick leads. Plant clues about tonight |
| 1:40-1:55 | **Scene 3: The roundup** (escalation at dusk) | Moral pressure, hand out a clue if they haven't found one |
| 1:55-2:40 | **Act 3: The cart** (the climax) | Stop the bomb, save Aiden, face complications |
| 2:40-2:55 | **Aftermath and advancement** | Consequences, reputation, drives, cliffhanger |

**For a 2-hour session:** shorten the intros, cut Scene 3 (use one clue delivery from section 6), and keep Act 2 to about 25 minutes. **For a 3-hour session:** add the optional scenes (Dubois's party, a fight at the Marquisate gate, or the ferryman's story).

**Golden rule:** if more than **5 minutes** pass with no one doing anything new, **make an escalation** (section 7) or **advance the clock**.

## 4. The clock (use it to create urgency)

Draw four boxes and tick one when time passes in the fiction, **or when a move misses**, **or when the scene drags**.

| Box | In the fiction | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Dusk** | The sun sets over the lake. Lanterns light | The soldiers begin Dubois's roundup of suspected sympathizers |
| **2. Nightfall** | Streets empty. Fog on the water | Aiden meets Hookpaw's contact and loads the crates onto a cart. **Tilda (added)** is ready to talk if approached |
| **3. Midnight** | Quiet streets, one moon | Aiden starts for the hospital with the cart |
| **4. Boom** | | The bomb goes off. If the PCs haven't stopped it, see "If they fail" (section 9) |

**Rules of thumb:**
- Only tick the clock **when it is dramatically right**. Don't do it silently.
- When you tick it, **say it aloud**: "The church bell strikes eight... Dusk falls."
- If PCs are efficient, they can **reach the cart before Midnight**. Everything is more manageable with warning.

## 5. The scenes

### Scene 1: Dawn at the docks (10 min)

**Goal:** welcome them, show who the factions are, show the stakes, introduce Daisy and Montague.

**Read-aloud (adapt it!):**
> The *Twice Sunk* creeps toward Bertram's Cove, a smudge of smoke and masts against a pink dawn. The air is salt and drying fish, tar and breakfast fires. Fishing boats crowd the harbor. Out in the open water, one big Marquisate brig, *Le Grande Tigre*, sits scarred and silent under repair. Beyond it, the Grand Lake stretches until you can't see land. When your ship bumps the inspection dock, you notice the posters: on every post, every wall, the same face. Red eyes. Scars. Fangs. **"CAPTAIN SPARROWHAWK. WANTED DEAD OR ALIVE."** Past the posters, a gleaming-armored cat is chewing out a squad of soldiers.

**What to do:**
1. **Daisy Delaware** (dockmaster) strides up the plank to collect harbor tax **and gossip**. She's friendly, fast, hungry for news. She is **neutral** and happy to introduce them to either side.
2. **Commandant Astrid Montague** notices the newcomers. She is curt but honorable. She wants to know: *Who are you? Have you seen Sparrowhawk's ships? What's your business?* A good chance for a **Persuade** or **Trick**, or **Figure Someone Out**.
3. Ask each player **one** question: "What does your character notice first?" or "Who do you recognize?" (see `05-character-hooks.md`).
4. Close with **"What do you do?"**

**Info to hand out (all easy, no roll needed):**
- Locals mostly **like** Sparrowhawk, but won't say so loudly near soldiers.
- The Marquisate offers a **big bounty**: a full resupply, a **sack of gold (12-Value)**, and a **pass** through any Marquisate territory.
- Saffron Goodacre is "the treasurer and historian, a good person to ask about local trouble."
- There is a **missing ship's treasure** rumor and a **missing spy** rumor (seeds for later).

### Scene 2: Fire on the water (25 min)

**Goal:** first action scene. Teach the dice. Get all six players doing something. Build prestige.

**The moment.** While they are still at the dock, a Marquisate **supply ship loaded with medical supplies** (for the hospital) pushes off, then **erupts in flames**. Sailors jump overboard. With nobody at the wheel, a gust catches the sails and the burning ship **drifts toward the moored fishing fleet**. Alarm bells ring. Montague shouts for help.

**Possible tasks (give each player something to try):**

| Task | Fits | Move to use |
|---|---|---|
| Swing or leap onto the burning ship | Harrier, Thief, Ranger | **Swinging Ship to Ship** (Finesse). Special move from the module |
| Grab the wheel and steer it away | Anyone | **Trust Fate** (Luck) or Might / Finesse if they argue it |
| Haul sailors out of the water | Arbiter | **Guardian** (if they have it), or **Trust Fate** |
| Cut mooring lines so the fishing boats can scatter | Tinker, Ranger | **Attempt a Roguish Feat** (Disable Device if they ask to rig something) or **Trust Fate** |
| Calm panicking townsfolk and organize a bucket line | Adventurer | **Persuade an NPC** |
| Save the **medical supplies** | Whoever's curious | **Trust Fate**, or a roguish feat |
| Find out who set the fire | Anyone | **Read a Tense Situation** |

**Outcomes:**
- **Full success:** the ship burns out harmlessly or runs aground. The **medical supplies are saved**. The party gains **1 Prestige with the Marquisate** and **1 with the Denizens**.
- **Partial:** the fleet takes damage, one fishing boat is lost, or they save sailors but lose the supplies. Montague thanks them while demanding to know *why* they were there.
- **Failure/ignored:** the fleet burns. The hospital is now **short of medicine**, which pressures the town later.

**Clue to plant here (pick one):** a sailor mutters "it was an Alliance sabotage... a little keg that shouldn't have been aboard," or the PCs **find the burnt remains of an incendiary device**. It's a signal that the Alliance is escalating tonight.

### Act 2: Free investigation (45 min)

Now let the players choose. Offer **three leads**. They can split up, but have **at most 2 groups**, and cut between them every few minutes. **Every lead plants a clue about tonight.**

#### Lead A: Daisy's workshop and the docks (info broker)
- She's neutral, curious, and open for a **trade** (information for information, or a favor). She is also trying to find a **team to dive for the Wavebreaker's gold** (side thread). She'll tempt the PCs with the **experimental diving rig**.
- **Clue she can give:** *"A cargo of blanket crates arrived for the hospital, but the cart was heavy. Heavier than blankets. Someone paid for it to be sent off the books."* She **inspected the crates** and didn't open them, but noticed the **acrid smell** on the cart wheel.
- A **Tinker** can bond with her over gadgets. A **Thief** can **notice what she's holding back**.

#### Lead B: The Marquisate fort (Montague and Dubois)
- **Montague** will talk if PCs offer **useful information**. She suspects the **armory or docks** are the target. She hasn't considered the hospital. She'll offer **coin, access, or favors** for useful intel.
- **Dubois** invites them to a **party** at the fort (optional scene): food, wine, and probing questions. He wants the PCs to find **Michel Lambert**, his missing deep-cover agent. If the PCs share a clue about crates or Aiden, he gives them his own: *"Three nights ago we followed a young mouse who bought lamp oil, black powder, and fuses in different shops. We lost him by the pier."*
- **Clue:** the fort has **heard an attack is imminent** (from the module), and Dubois's agents saw **someone loading crates on a cart behind the Fishmonger's (added)**, a lead to the cart.

#### Lead C: The town and the rebels
- **Fishermen and townsfolk** quietly love Sparrowhawk. Ask about him and they smile, then glance at soldiers.
- **Saffron Goodacre** receives them in her cozy study: warm, curious, generous. She asks them about their travels and subtly **tests whether they might be allies**. She can be **very persuasive**. Don't reveal her secret; **let the PCs wonder**.
- **Aiden Birch** is easy to find: he's **boasting in a tavern** about "the night Sparrowhawk's people shake the Cove". He's drinking a little too much and **wants someone to be impressed**. A **Harrier** or **Ranger** can follow him.
- **Tilda Fenn (added):** a nervous rebel dockhand from Saffron's inner circle who has **doubts about the plan**. Hint to the PCs through a fishermen's whisper: *"Ask Tilda at the net loft what she's afraid of."* She's afraid of the **bomb**, and will talk **only if the PCs are trusted** (kindness, a good cover story, or a reputation with the Alliance).
- **Clue:** Tilda reveals: *"They're going to blow up the hospital tonight. The kid doesn't know how. I don't know how to stop it."*

> **Three clue rule (important for beginners):** whatever the players do, **at least three separate sources** should point to "something happens at the hospital tonight." If they miss them, **have Tilda (added) find them**, or have **Daisy tug on their sleeve** with the crate story. The module intends the PCs to be able to *miss* this and see consequences. For a first game, make sure they get a fair chance.

**Reputation moves in Act 2:**
- When meeting someone important (Montague, Dubois, Saffron), they can use **Meet Someone Important** (roll + Reputation with that faction). Early on, reputations are low, so it's a quick teaching moment.
- **Ask for a Favor** works if they earned **prestige** in the fire scene.

### Scene 3: The roundup (dusk escalation, 15 min)

**Trigger:** Tick the clock to **Dusk**.
**What happens:** Montague's soldiers, under **Dubois's** order, start **dragging suspected sympathizers from their homes** into the street, demanding the location of the Alliance base. Neighbors scream. Unarmed townsfolk can't stop them.

**Why it matters:** this is the moral beat. Whatever the PCs do will have **ripples**:
- Stop it by **talking down the soldiers** (Persuade, Orator, Carry a Big Stick, Fast Friends)
- Stop it by **distracting them** (Thief, Harrier, Adventurer's Fast Friends)
- Join in? Feeds **Marquisate prestige** but **Denizens notoriety**
- Take advantage: **sneak into an empty shop**, find **Aiden's cart receipt** (**clue**)

**Deliver one more clue here if they still don't know:** a captured sympathizer, panicked, shouts: **"It's the HOSPITAL. They'll burn the hospital!"** or a child hands them a **note**: *"Tilda says come to the net loft."*

### Act 3: The cart (climax, 45 min)

**Setup.** By Nightfall or Midnight, the PCs are heading to the hospital district. The streets are dark and foggy. A cart loaded with **blanket crates** rattles along the cobbles. **Aiden** pushes it, torch sputtering in one paw, pipe in his mouth, a book of stories jammed in his pocket. A **barrel** is hidden in the crates, **unmarked**, with an **acrid smell**.

**Three problems happen at once.** (Hand a different one to a different player. Cut between them.)

1. **Aiden and the cart (the heart).** He is terrified and proud. The bomb is **almost ready**, but he can't arm it properly, and **the torch or his pipe ember is close to the barrel**.
2. **The soldiers.** A **Marquisate squad** (small mob) patrols nearby. If they see Aiden's cart near the hospital, they'll **rush** to inspect, and might **start a fight** with PCs. **Dubois is also watching** and might step in.
3. **The ambush.** **Alliance rebels** (small mob) are hidden in alleys near the hospital, waiting for the blast to hit the responders. If the PCs interfere, they might **jump out**. Hookpaw leads them, or sends a message.

**How the PCs can solve each problem** (all valid; reward creativity):

| Problem | Ways |
|---|---|
| **Aiden** | **Persuade an NPC** (a strong motive: a way to be brave without killing people). **Trick an NPC** (a harmless distraction). **Figure Someone Out** (what does he wish I'd do?). **Orator** or **Fast Friends** (Adventurer). Shame him, inspire him, or offer him a braver job than killing people. He's scared underneath. A **7-9** on Persuade means he **stalls** but demands that the PCs prove they have a better plan. |
| **The bomb** | **Disable Device** (roguish feat). **Toolbox** (Tinker: GM sets 1-4 conditions, e.g. *you need quiet, a clear table, and 10 minutes*). **Jury Rig.** **Pick Lock** the crate. **Wreck Something** (Might) to destroy the barrel, collateral risk. **Trust Fate** to dump the cart into the harbor. **Poisons and Antidotes** (Ranger) for a chemical solution. Armor-clad Arbiter can **Guardian** bystanders. |
| **Soldiers** | **Persuade** or **Trick** to delay them, **Carry a Big Stick** to hold a standoff, **Confuse Senses** to flee, or **fight** (see below). |
| **Rebels** | **Read a Tense Situation** to spot them early. **Harry a Group** to scatter them. **Persuade** Hookpaw **only** with a very strong motive. |

**Complications to throw in (on a 7-9 or a miss):**
- The **barrel starts leaking** onto the cobbles: **any open flame** is now dangerous.
- The torch **falls** (or Aiden panics and drops it).
- A **patrolling soldier** shouts and everyone **freezes**.
- The **hospital door opens** and a nurse with a lantern steps out.
- **Montague** shows up (she had a bad feeling). She orders them to stand down. Everyone's wrong about what's happening.
- **Hookpaw** steps from the dark, **furious** and holding his hook.
- A **fire** begins. Now there's a **race** to evacuate patients **and** fight the fire.

**Combat in this game (if it happens):**
- Keep it **short and cinematic**. Mix **weapon moves** with **narration**.
- Use **morale**. Most NPCs will break, flee, or surrender.
- Use **small mob** stats for squads (3 of each track, x2 harm) from `03-npcs-and-places.md`. It's fast.
- **Give PCs interesting choices**, not just "I hit him." Use Engage in Melee's pick list, Parry, Disarm, Storm a Group, Harry.
- **Don't let one player win the fight alone.** The Arbiter is a bruiser. Ask the Harrier "What are you doing while Katie holds the line?"

**Saffron at the end.** If the PCs end up face to face with Saffron (optional), she's calm and kind. She'll **try to recruit or manipulate** them. A good reveal: she **knew** the PCs were in town and **tested them**.

## 6. If the PCs miss the clues (safety valves)

Pick one, **before the clock hits Midnight**:

1. **Daisy drags them aside**: "I think that cart is going to the hospital, and it smells wrong."
2. **Tilda (added)** intercepts the most sympathetic PC: **tearful, scared**, asks for help.
3. **Aiden is seen** at the dock, **loud and drunk**: "You'll see! Tonight, Sparrowhawk's people..."
4. **Dubois's agent** hands them a **note** (or a bribe) saying "A boy bought black powder in three shops."
5. **A smell** (acrid, chemical) drifts across the street, then a **crash** of something rolling off a cart.
6. Use the module's escalation: a quiet moonlit street, Aiden's wheelbarrow topples, a **flammable barrel** cracks open. The PCs are **just there**.

## 7. Escalation menu (what to throw in when it goes quiet)

From the module (paraphrased), plus our own **(added)**:

- **Wheelbarrow in the moonlit street:** Aiden's barrow hits a stone, an **unmarked barrel** cracks, an acrid, flammable fluid spills. *(The module's classic.)*
- **The roundup:** soldiers drag suspected sympathizers into the street; unarmed neighbors can't stop them.
- **The burning supply ship:** it drifts toward the moored fishing fleet. *(Use as Scene 2.)*
- **Sparrowhawk sighted:** a **jaunty red hat** flashes down an alley. The docks erupt. *(Good decoy; could be Kell, or a decoy, or even Declan.)*
- **(added) The password changes:** the fort's night password is changed without notice. A PC **inside** has **three minutes** to get out.
- **(added) A rumor of reward:** a **bounty hunter** offers to sell the PCs a lead on **Sparrowhawk's location**.
- **(added) A hospital visitor:** a **sick sailor** staggers out, coughing, and begs the PCs to take a letter for his child.

## 9. Outcomes (and how to end the session)

Don't judge. Let the story land.

### If they succeed (the bomb never goes off)
- **Aiden lives** and either runs away, joins the PCs, turns himself in, or is **protected by them**.
- **Prestige:** **Denizens** (+1 or +2), and possibly **Marquisate** or **Alliance**, depending on how they did it.
- **Consequence:** they **know Saffron's secret**. They **hold power**. What do they do? Tell Montague (Marquisate prestige, Alliance notoriety, Saffron arrested?), tell no one, confront Saffron, or use it.

### If they partly succeed
- The bomb **goes off in the street, not in the hospital**. Some damage, a few injuries, but no mass casualties. Montague **suspects everyone**.

### If they fail
- **The module's default:** a premature explosion kills **Aiden**, injures bystanders, lights a **terrible fire**. **Montague places the Cove under martial law and bans fishing.** The town starves and must forage or buy from Marquisate markets.
- The PCs still have **a story**: they know who did it and who's responsible.

### Cliffhangers for next session (pick one)
- **Dubois** corners them: *"Where is Michel Lambert?"* (he knows they have connections)
- **Daisy** shows up at dawn: *"I know where the Wavebreaker went down. I need six strong backs and a boat."*
- **Declan** appears with a letter: *"I think someone is about to get Kell killed."*
- **Hookpaw** has found out who stopped his plan.
- **Montague** offers the **Sparrowhawk bounty**, 12-Value in gold and a pass, to anyone who can bring her the truth.

### Advancement
After the session, check each **drive** (the condition on their sheet). Anyone who met a condition **advances**: pick a +1 stat, a new move, two weapon skills, two feats, +1 box on a track, or new connections. At most **once per drive per session**.

## 10. Side threads for session two and beyond

*These come straight from the module's other conflicts.*

### To Catch a Spy
- **Michel Lambert**, Dubois's deep-cover agent, is imprisoned at the **secret Alliance base** with other suspects. **Hookpaw** has been interrogating them. If no answers come, **Hookpaw and Saffron will kill three suspects**, Michel among them, and spread a rumor they were traitors.
- Michel holds **proof that Saffron is the Alliance leader** and knows the base's location, guard shifts, and fortifications.
- *Seed:* Dubois asks for help, or a prisoner's relative begs for it.

### Treasure in the Depths
- The courier ship **Wavebreaker** was attacked by pirates and scuttled in deep water. Rumor: a **military payroll chest** and **classified documents in the captain's quarters**. The **Marquisate and Alliance each offer 20%**.
- **Daisy** has a likely location and an **experimental diving rig**; she wants a **50/50 split**.
- Use **Deepwater Recovery** (see `02-rules-reference.md`).
- Both factions' **patrols skirmish** in the area.
- *Seed:* Daisy's pitch in Act 2.

### Love and Rebellion
- **Kell Sweetgrass** plays the "monstrous" Sparrowhawk. He wants to go home to his fiance **Declan**; Saffron has convinced him that going home would get his family killed.
- If nothing changes, Kell is **captured in a raid** and **hanged**. Declan's silent vigil moves the town to demand peace.
- *Seed:* the Sparrowhawk posters, Declan's tree-knot letters, a **jaunty red hat**.

## 11. Troubleshooting and ruling guidance

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **The players are quiet or unsure** | Ask directly: "Marline, what does your character do?" Offer **2 or 3 choices**. Use **a carrot or a stick** |
| **They want to do something clever and off-script** | **Say yes.** Ask "How?" Call for a roll if there's risk |
| **A rule question stops the game** | Make a quick ruling. Note it. Look it up **after** |
| **A player dominates** | Narrate to someone else: "Meanwhile, Ben, you see..." |
| **They split up too much** | Cut between groups **every 2-3 minutes** |
| **They want to join the Alliance** | Great! Saffron is delighted and offers a **role**. Now they face the bomb as insiders. Tilda still helps them see the problem |
| **They want to side with the Marquisate** | Great! Dubois and Montague offer a **role**. The raid and their reputation matter |
| **A PC is knocked out** | Give them a **quick scene** (a dream, a prisoner's conversation) or let their player **run an NPC** (Aiden!) |
| **They ask "Can I do X?"** | "What does your character do?" and "Which move does that sound like?" |
| **Combat is dragging** | Make **morale** break. Have the enemy flee or surrender |
| **The scene is too heavy** | Add a moment of **levity**: Daisy's absurd gadget, Aiden's book of stories, a misprinted poster |

### Ruling cheat phrases
- "Roll with **[stat]**."
- "On a 7-9, you do it, **but** [cost]."
- "On a miss... **hold on, what does your character feel?**" (then make a move)
- "That would be **Trust Fate**."

### Tone tips
- **Silly and dramatic.** Mice and squirrels in armor, a villain with a notebook and a bottle of wine, a hero who's a nervous fisherman in a costume.
- **Play every NPC as if they think they're the hero.** Montague, Saffron, Dubois, Hookpaw, and Aiden each believe they're protecting something.
- The **best moments** come from **misses** turned into **cool complications**.
