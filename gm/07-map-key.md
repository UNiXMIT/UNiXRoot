# Bertram's Cove Map Key

The module has no town map. Locations marked **(added)** are ours. Part 1 is safe to print beside the player map. Part 2 is GM only.

## Part 2: GM key (spoilers)

### 1. Inspection Dock
- **Sights and smells:** tar, salt, drying fish, breakfast fires. Brightly colored wanted posters on every piling.
- **Who:** Daisy (collecting tax and gossip), Montague (dressing down soldiers).
- **Use:** Scene 1, the start of Scene 2 (the burning supply ship leaves from here).

### 2. Harbor and Moorings
- **Sights:** fishing vessels thick in the cove; *Le Grande Tigre* quiet under repair.
- **Use:** the burning ship drifts toward the fleet. Swinging Ship to Ship, cutting mooring lines, hauling sailors out.

### 3. Marquisate Fort
- **Sights:** thick wooden fortifications, one central gate, an inner courtyard with small wooden buildings, training areas, a command structure and a long rectangular barracks. Catwalks along the walls, patrolled day and night.
- **Night rule:** access needs **the day's password**.
- **Who:** Montague, Dubois, about 70 soldiers and staff.
- **Use:** Lead B. Dubois's party. A sneak or heist target for later sessions.

### 4. Daisy's Workshop
- **Sights:** a mess of half-finished projects, stacked materials and books, except for an immaculate workbench and a lovingly organized tool rack. An oil lamp hangs by the bench.
- **Security:** a simple padlock.
- **Use:** Lead A. Gadgets, the **untested diving rig**, a Tinker's playground.

### 5. Hospital **(details added)**
- **Truth:** a Marquisate-funded hospital that treats **all** denizens. A **company of sailors** is quarantined here with the **Cuttlefish Cough**.
- **Details (added):** a long low building with a courtyard, a delivery door at the side for crates and blankets, a storeroom, and wards. A nurse with a lantern may step out at night.
- **Use:** the **climax**. Where the cart is headed.

### 6. The Drowned Lantern **(added)**
- **Sights:** low beams, wet boots, a fire, fishermen and off-duty sailors.
- **Who:** **Aiden Birch**, drinking a little too much, boasting about "the night Sparrowhawk's people shake the Cove".
- **Use:** Lead C. A Harrier or Ranger can tail him. A **clue source** for the three clue rule.

### 7. Fish Market **(added)**
- **Sights:** ice, barrels, gulls, crates stacked behind the stalls.
- **Truth:** Dubois's agents saw someone **loading crates onto a cart behind the Fishmonger's**. This is where the bomb cart starts.
- **Use:** a clue (Lead B), a place for a stakeout, the start of the cart's route.

### 8. Net Loft **(added)**
- **Sights:** nets hung from the rafters, coils of rope, shuttles and floats. Quiet.
- **Who:** **Tilda Fenn**, a nervous rebel dockhand with doubts about the plan.
- **Use:** Lead C. She talks only if the PCs earn her trust.

### 9. Market Street **(added)**
- **Sights:** stalls, shopfronts, a chandler, an apothecary.
- **Truth:** Aiden bought **lamp oil, black powder and fuses in three separate shops**.
- **Use:** the roundup (Scene 3). Soldiers drag suspects from houses. The cart's route to the hospital passes through here, narrow and foggy at night.

### 10. Bell Tower **(added)**
- **Use:** your **clock**. Ring it aloud: "The bell strikes eight. Dusk falls." Makes time feel real and gives a safe moment to pause the table.

### 11. Town Hall **(added)**
- **Who:** **Saffron Goodacre**, treasurer and historian, in a cozy study. Her notebook is on the desk.
- **Truth:** Saffron's **war journal** is kept in a hidden drawer with a false bottom. It's the document that tipped her off to a spy (part of the Michel storyline).
- **Use:** Lead C. A heist target for a Thief in session two.

### 12. Woods and Rocky Shore
- **Sights:** jagged rocks, old-growth trees, a short **granite canyon** leading inland from a beach.
- **Truth:** the **secret Alliance base** is hidden here. Fishing vessels crewed by Alliance sympathizers patrol the water nearby.
- **Use:** foreshadowing now (a Ranger may spot patrol boats), a major location for session two.

### Hidden places (leave off the player map)

| Place | Why it's hidden | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Secret Alliance base** | Hidden among rocks and trees; invisible from the lake. Camouflaged harbor down the shore | Michel's prison, Saffron's stronghold, Kell hiding as Sparrowhawk |
| **Wreck of the *Wavebreaker*** | Deep in the lake, dark, on its side with a hole in the hull. Classified documents in the captain's quarters, aft | Treasure thread. Needs the diving rig and **Deepwater Recovery** |
| **Kell's family home** | Declan lives with Kell's parents | Love and Rebellion thread |
| **Old tree with a knot** | Kell and Declan leave each other notes in it | Love and Rebellion thread |

### Quick placement tips
- Keep the **Fish Market, Market Street and hospital** close together so the cart route is short enough to run in a scene.
- Put the **Net Loft** by the water, away from soldiers.
- Put the **bell tower** where everyone can see it.
- Don't draw the **Alliance base** or the **wreck**; just say "off the map" if the players ask.
