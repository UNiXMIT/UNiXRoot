# Nick: The Thief

*A cunning, criminal vagabond who can steal even the most well-guarded treasures.*

**Suggested quick build.** You can change anything.

## Stats (roll 2d6 + stat)

| Charm | Cunning | Finesse | Luck | Might |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 0 | **+1** | **+2** | +1 | -1 |

*(Playbook starts 0 / 0 / +2 / +1 / -1. Suggested +1 goes to Cunning.)*

## Your moves (choose 3)

| Move | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Breaking and Entering** | You attempt roguish feats to get into or out of a place you've **been before** | You can **mark exhaustion** to treat the roll as a **10+** without rolling |
| **Disappear Into the Dark** | You slip into shadows while unnoticed. Mark **exhaustion**, hold 1 | While you remain quiet and slow, you stay hidden. Lose the hold if you reveal yourself. Spend the hold to **reveal yourself suddenly**. If you **attack** right after, **+3 on the roll** |
| **Nose for Gold** | You **Figure Someone Out** or **Read a Tense Situation** | You can always ask (even on a miss): *what's the most valuable thing they're carrying?* (Figure Someone Out) / *what's the most valuable thing here?* (Read a Tense Situation) |

*(Other options: Rope-a-Dope (tire an enemy by dodging), Small Hands (grapple with Finesse), Master Thief (+1 Finesse).)*

## Roguish feats (choose 4)
Suggested: **Sneak**, **Pick Lock**, **Pickpocket**, **Hide**. (Also: Acrobatics, Blindside, Counterfeit, Disable Device, Sleight of Hand.)

## Weapon skill (choose one)
**Confuse Senses**: throw something at close or intimate range. Roll **Finesse**. On a hit, they're off-balance, blinded, deafened, or confused, and you get an **opportunity**. **10+** they need time to recover; **7-9** you get a few moments.
*(Alternatives: Parry, Trick Shot, Improvise Weapon. Parry pairs with the dagger.)*

## Equipment (starting value 6; suggested spend: 5, 1 left as coin)

| Item | Wear | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Dagger** (5) | 1 | Intimate / close. **Parry, Vicious Strike.** *Quick:* mark exhaustion to melee with **Finesse** instead of Might |

Load: 0 of 3 before being burdened (max 6). The dagger counts as 0 Load. You travel light. That's the point.

## Nature (choose one)
- **Kleptomaniac:** *Clear your exhaustion when you try to selfishly steal something valuable or important.*
- **Rebellious:** Clear your exhaustion when you grievously insult, defy, or anger figures of authority.

## Drives (choose two)
- **Greed:** advance when you secure a serious payday or treasure.
- **Thrills:** advance when you escape from certain death or incarceration.
- *(Also available: Freedom, Ambition.)*

## Connections (suggested)
- **Professional: Michelle's Harrier.** You stole something important for someone; you proved your worth. When you share info after **Read a Tense Situation**, you both get +1. When you **help** them with a roguish feat, you get the 2-exhaustion options for 1 exhaustion.
- **Friend: Ben's Ranger.** They sprang you from holding. You owe them. When you help them, mark **2 exhaustion for +2**.

## Your tie to Bertram's Cove (optional)
You've heard a **rumor**: a Marquisate courier ship went down carrying **something valuable**, and both sides are hunting it.

## Quick tips
- You're the **sneak**, the **lockpick**, and the **pickpocket**. When there's a lock, a guard, or a pocket, it's you.
- When you **Attempt a Roguish Feat**, say your **goal** first ("I'm sneaking into the barracks").
- At **7-9** you **succeed** but mark **exhaustion or a risk** (detection, evidence). Pick the cheaper cost.
- **Nose for Gold** gives you **free info** on every Read or Figure Out.
- Your **Might** is weak (-1). Avoid straight fights. **Confuse Senses** or slip away.
