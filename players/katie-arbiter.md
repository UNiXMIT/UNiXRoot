# Katie: The Arbiter

*A powerful, obstinate vagabond somewhere between a mercenary and a protector, perhaps taking sides too easily in the greater conflict.*

**Suggested quick build.** You can change anything.

## Stats (roll 2d6 + stat)

| Charm | Cunning | Finesse | Luck | Might |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| +1 | **+1** | 0 | -1 | **+2** |

*(Playbook starts +1 / 0 / 0 / -1 / +2. Suggested +1 goes to Cunning so you can read a situation. No stat above +2 at creation.)*

## Your moves (choose 3)

| Move | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Guardian** | You defend someone or something from an immediate NPC or environmental threat (roll **Might**) | **Hit:** you keep them safe and choose one: draw the threat's attention to you / put the threat in a vulnerable spot (+1 forward to counterstrike) / push the threat back so you can maneuver or flee. **7-9:** it costs you: expose yourself to danger or escalate. **Miss:** you take the full blow and the threat has you |
| **Carry a Big Stick** | You use words to pause an argument or violent conflict between others (roll **Charm**) | **Hit:** they choose: mark **2 exhaustion** and keep going, or stop for now. **10+:** +1 ongoing to dealing with them peacefully. **Miss:** NPCs turn their anger on you; PCs get +1 ongoing against you for the scene |
| **Hardy** | always | **+1 injury box.** Whenever time passes or you reach a new clearing, **clear 2 injury** automatically |

*(Other options: Brute (+1 Might, up to +3), Crash and Smash (smash through scenery with Might), Strong Draw (powerful bow shots).)*

## Roguish feat (choose 1)
Suggested: **Acrobatics** (climb, vault, swing). Any one is fine.

## Weapon skill (choose one)
**Storm a Group**: pairs with the battle axe. Mark exhaustion, roll **Might**. On a hit, trade harm. **10+** choose 2, **7-9** choose 1: **2 morale harm** / **2 exhaustion on them** / **suffer less harm** / **they hurt each other** (mark exhaustion again).
*(Alternatives: Cleave, Parry, Disarm.)*

## Equipment (starting value 10; suggested spend: 10)

| Item | Wear | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Battle Axe** (7) | 4 | Close. **Storm a Group, Cleave** (you learn Cleave later). *Large:* mark exhaustion when inflicting harm to inflict **1 extra** harm |
| **Chainmail** (3) | 3 | *Tightly woven:* after a fight, spend a few seconds to clear 1 wear. *Weighty:* counts as +1 Load |

Load: 3 of 6 before being burdened (max 12).

## Nature (choose one)
- **Defender:** *Clear your exhaustion when you put yourself in harm's way to defend someone against injustice or dire threat.*
- **Punisher:** Clear your exhaustion when you tell a powerful or dangerous villain to their face that you will punish them.

## Drives (choose two)
- **Justice:** advance when you achieve justice for someone wronged by a powerful, wealthy, or high-status individual.
- **Principles:** advance when you express or embody your moral principles at great cost to yourself or your allies.
- *(Also available: Loyalty (name them), Protection (name your ward).)*

## Connections (suggested)
- **Partner: Marline's Adventurer.** Together you helped a faction take control of a clearing. Each marks **2 prestige** with the faction you helped and **2 notoriety** with the faction you harmed. When **spotted together**, gains are **doubled**.
- **Protector: Noah's Tinker.** When they're in reach, **mark exhaustion to take a blow meant for them**. Then you get **+1 ongoing to weapon moves** for the scene.

## Your tie to Bertram's Cove (optional)
Years ago you were hired by **Commandant Astrid Montague** (or a commander in her unit) as an armed escort. She paid honestly. You don't know whether she remembers you.

## Quick tips
- You're the **muscle** and the **protector**. When trouble starts, step in front of friends.
- **Guardian** and **Carry a Big Stick** let you **solve problems without a fight**. Try them.
- Your **Luck** is poor (-1). Avoid **Trust Fate** unless you must.
- You're **tough** (Hardy), but don't be reckless. Armor can soak damage.
