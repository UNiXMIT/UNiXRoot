# Ben: The Ranger

*A capable, stealthy vagabond centered on the forests between the clearings, more interested in the wilds than in company.*

**Suggested quick build.** You can change anything.

## Stats (roll 2d6 + stat)

| Charm | Cunning | Finesse | Luck | Might |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| -1 | +1 | **+2** | 0 | +1 |

*(Playbook starts -1 / +1 / +1 / 0 / +1. Suggested +1 goes to Finesse.)*

## Your moves (choose 3)

| Move | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Silent Paws** | You attempt a roguish feat to **sneak or hide** | Mark **2 exhaustion** to turn a **miss into a 7-9** |
| **Slip Away** | You take advantage of an opening to escape (roll **Finesse**) | **Hit:** you get away. **10+** choose 1, **7-9** choose 2: you suffer injury or exhaustion / you end up in another dangerous situation / you leave something important behind. **Miss:** you escape but it costs you, and you leave tracks |
| **Poisons and Antidotes** | You brew a poison (mark **depletion**, say the effect: sleep, weakness, inebriation, or death) or study a poison to brew an **antidote** | Needs ingestion or injection. The GM tells you the special ingredient you need for an antidote (then mark depletion to brew it) |

*(Other options: Forager (clear depletion/exhaustion/injury when passing through a forest), Threatening Visage (Persuade with Might), Dirty Fighter (two extra weapon skills).)*

## Roguish feats (already marked)
**Sneak** and **Hide**.

## Weapon skill (choose one)
**Vicious Strike**: pairs with the dagger. Mark exhaustion, roll **Might**. On a hit, **serious harm** and they can't use **armor** to absorb it. **10+** clean; **7-9** they hit you back.
*(Alternatives: Cleave, Disarm, Harry a Group.)*

## Equipment (starting value 9; suggested spend: 8, 1 left as coin)

| Item | Wear | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Dagger** (5) | 1 | Intimate / close. **Parry, Vicious Strike.** *Quick:* mark exhaustion to melee with **Finesse** instead of Might |
| **Leather Armor** (3) | 2 | *Flexible:* when grappling, mark exhaustion to ignore the foe's first choice |

Load: 1 of 5 before being burdened (max 10). The dagger counts as 0 Load.

## Nature (choose one)
- **Cynic:** *Clear your exhaustion when you openly and directly ask dangerous questions about an accepted "truth".*
- **Loner:** Clear your exhaustion when you enter a dangerous situation alone, without backup or assistance.

## Drives (choose two)
- **Discovery:** advance when you encounter a new wonder or ruin in the forests.
- **Freedom:** advance when you free a group of denizens from oppression.
- *(Also available: Revenge (name a foe), Protection (name a ward).)*

## Connections (suggested)
- **Watcher: Nick's Thief.** They once tricked you. When you **Figure Them Out**, you always hold **1 even on a miss**. When you **Plead** with them, they can clear **2 exhaustion** instead of 1.
- **Protector: Marline's Adventurer.** When they're in reach, **mark exhaustion to take a blow meant for them**. Then you get **+1 ongoing to weapon moves** for the scene.

## Your tie to Bertram's Cove (optional)
You've camped in the **rocky, old-growth woods** along the lake's eastern shore. You know the area is full of **hidden coves and smugglers' trails**, and you don't trust anyone with a flag.

## Quick tips
- You're the **tracker**. Use **Read a Tense Situation** (Cunning) to spot ambushes and escape routes.
- You're **terrible at charm** (-1). Let someone else talk.
- **Sneak/Hide** is your bread and butter. **Silent Paws** saves you from a miss, but costs 2 exhaustion. Use it only when it matters.
- Your **Poisons and Antidotes** are a surprising tool. Think about what you could brew.
