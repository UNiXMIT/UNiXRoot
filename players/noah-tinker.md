# Noah: The Tinker

*An adept, clever vagabond interested in mechanisms and craftsmanship, perhaps possessed of ideas that set you apart.*

**Suggested quick build.** You can change anything.

## Stats (roll 2d6 + stat)

| Charm | Cunning | Finesse | Luck | Might |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| -1 | **+2** | **+2** | 0 | 0 |

*(Playbook starts -1 / +2 / +1 / 0 / 0. Suggested +1 goes to Finesse.)*

## Your moves

You always have **Toolbox** and **Repair**, and you pick one more.

| Move | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Toolbox** | You open your toolkit and dedicate yourself to making something or getting to the bottom of something | Tell the GM what. The GM gives you **1 to 4 conditions** (time, materials, help, tools or facilities, limits). When you meet them all, you accomplish your goal. *Pick two features: gears and springs / esoteric hand tools / manuals / sewing kit / etc. Pick one drawback: heavy, bulky and obvious, stolen, fragile* |
| **Repair** | You repair gear with your toolbox | **Damaged** gear: spend **depletion or Value 1-for-1** for each box of wear cleared. **Destroyed** gear: the GM sets one condition, then you clear all wear |
| **Jury Rig** (suggested third) | You create a makeshift device on the fly (roll **Cunning**) | **Hit:** it works **once**, then breaks. **10+** choose: it works exceptionally well / you get an extra use. **Miss:** it works, but with an unintended side effect |

*(Other options: Big Pockets (+2 depletion boxes), Nimble Mind (use Cunning for mechanisms and locks, mark depletion), Dismantle (clear 2 depletion).)*

## Roguish feats (already marked)
**Counterfeit**, **Disable Device** (disarm traps and mechanisms), and **Pick Lock**.

## Weapon skill (choose one)
**Improvise Weapon**: roll **Cunning**, no weapon needed. On a hit you make a weapon out of what's around; the GM gives it a range and a helpful tag. **7-9:** it also has a weakness.
*(Alternatives: Cleave, Harry a Group, Trick Shot.)*

## Equipment (starting value 8; suggested spend: 3, keep 5 as coin and supplies)

| Item | Wear | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Toolbox** | n/a | Your kit (counts as 1 Load) |
| **Leather Armor** (3) | 2 | *Flexible:* when grappling, mark exhaustion to ignore the foe's first choice |
| **5 Value in coin** | 5 | Spend it a box at a time, e.g. to **repair** things or buy parts |

Load: 2 of 4 before being burdened (max 8).

## Nature (choose one)
- **Perfectionist:** *Clear your exhaustion when you replace someone else's existing tool or resource with something truly great.*
- **Radical:** Clear your exhaustion when you espouse dangerous ideas to the wrong audience.

## Drives (choose two)
- **Ambition:** advance when you increase your reputation with any faction.
- **Greed:** advance when you secure a serious payday or treasure.
- *(Also available: Revenge, Protection.)*

## Connections (suggested)
- **Professional: Michelle's Harrier.** When you share info after **Read a Tense Situation**, you both get +1. When you **help** them with a roguish feat, you get the 2-exhaustion help options for 1 exhaustion.
- **Family: Katie's Arbiter.** You had each other's back when you were run out of a clearing because your natures got out of hand. When you **help them fulfill their Nature**, you both **clear your exhaustion track**.

## Your tie to Bertram's Cove (optional)
You've traded letters with **Daisy Delaware**, the Cove's dockmaster, who writes in the margins of her gadget sketches. She says she's building something that needs a tinker's eye.

## Quick tips
- You're the **fixer**. When anyone says "Can we make that?" or "How does it work?", they look at you.
- **Toolbox** is a negotiation with the GM: ask "What would I need?" and the GM names the conditions.
- You're not a fighter. Use **Help or Interfere** and **Disable Device** to be useful.
- If you find a **trap**, **mechanism**, or **lock**, you're probably the answer.
