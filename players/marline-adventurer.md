# Marline: The Adventurer

*A peaceful, diplomatic vagabond who makes allies and topples powers through strong bonds.*

**Suggested quick build.** You can change anything. These choices are designed to be fun and easy in this first adventure.

## Stats (roll 2d6 + stat)

| Charm | Cunning | Finesse | Luck | Might |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **+2** | **+2** | 0 | 0 | -1 |

*(Playbook starts +2 / +1 / 0 / 0 / -1. Suggested +1 goes to Cunning. No stat can go above +2 at creation.)*

## Your moves (choose 3)

| Move | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Talon on the Pulse** | You gather information about what's going on in a clearing (roll **Cunning**) | **10+** ask 3, **7-9** ask 2: Who holds power here? / Who is the local dissident? / What are the denizens afraid of? / What do they hope for? / What opportunities exist for vagabonds? **Miss:** your questions tip off someone dangerous |
| **Fast Friends** | You try to befriend an NPC you've just met by matching their personality, body language and desires. Mark **exhaustion**, roll **Cunning** | **Hit:** they look on you favorably. Ask one non-compromising question (answered truthfully) **or** request a simple favor. **10+:** they share a **valuable secret** or grant a **serious favor** instead. **Miss:** you read them wrong and pay for it |
| **Orator** | You give a speech to interested denizens (roll **Charm**) | **Hit:** they move to do it as they see fit. **10+** choose 2, **7-9** choose 1: they don't take it too far / they don't disband at the first resistance / they don't demand you lead. **Miss:** they twist your message |

*(Other options: Sterling Reputation, Subduing Strikes (fight non-lethally with Cunning), Well-Read (+1 Cunning).)*

## Roguish feats (already marked)
**Counterfeit** (forgery, fakes) and **Sleight of Hand** (palming, switching).

## Weapon skill (choose one)
**Parry**: pairs with the short sword. Mark exhaustion, roll **Finesse**. On a hit you consume their attention. **10+** all three, **7-9** pick one: inflict morale or exhaustion / disarm your opponent / take no harm.

## Equipment (starting value 9; suggested spend: 8, 1 left as coin)

| Item | Wear | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mousefolk Short Sword** (5) | 2 | Close. **Parry, Disarm.** *Mousefolk Steel:* mark wear to melee with **Cunning** instead of Might |
| **Leather Armor** (3) | 2 | *Flexible:* when grappling, mark exhaustion to ignore the foe's first choice |

Load: 2 of 3 before being burdened (max 6).

## Nature (choose one)
- **Peacemaker:** *Clear your exhaustion when you resolve a dangerous conflict nonviolently.* (Great for this adventure.)
- **Extrovert:** Clear your exhaustion when you share a moment of real warmth, friendship, or enjoyment with someone.

## Drives (choose two)
- **Ambition:** advance when you increase your reputation with any faction.
- **Justice:** advance when you achieve justice for someone wronged by a powerful, wealthy, or high-status individual.
- *(Also available: Clean Paws, Principles.)*

## Connections (suggested)
- **Partner: Katie's Arbiter.** You fought together to defend a clearing and failed. Each of you marks **2 prestige** with the faction you helped and **2 notoriety** with the faction you harmed. When **spotted together**, your gains are **doubled**.
- **Friend: Ben's Ranger.** When you help them, you can mark **2 exhaustion for +2** (instead of 1 for +1).

## Your tie to Bertram's Cove (optional, pick or invent)
You grew up across the **Grand Lake** in **Oar's Rest**. As a child you listened to stories from a visitor from Bertram's Cove, **Saffron Goodacre**, who talked of adventurers toppling tyrants. You promised to visit someday.

## Reputation
Start with the blanks below, then note your background answers.
**Denizens** __ **Marquisate** __ **Alliance** __ (mark 2 prestige for the faction you served most; 1 notoriety for a faction you earned enmity with)

## Quick tips
- You're the **talker**: use **Persuade**, **Figure Someone Out**, and your moves in every scene.
- You're **fragile in a fight** (Might -1). Parry, talk, and let friends fight.
- **Help** your allies (mark exhaustion for +1).
- Remember **Fast Friends** on an important NPC you just met.
