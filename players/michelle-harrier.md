# Michelle: The Harrier

*A quick, enterprising vagabond who races from building to building and clearing to clearing, perhaps finding places others would rather keep hidden.*

**Suggested quick build.** You can change anything.

## Stats (roll 2d6 + stat)

| Charm | Cunning | Finesse | Luck | Might |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 0 | -1 | **+2** | **+2** | 0 |

*(Playbook starts 0 / -1 / +2 / +1 / 0. Suggested +1 goes to Luck. No stat above +2 at creation.)*

## Your moves (choose 3)

| Move | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Parkour** | You dash through a chaotic scene or fight (roll **Finesse**) | **10+** hold 3, **7-9** hold 2. Spend 1 hold each to **dash to something** within sight/reach without being stopped, or **dash away** from something nearby, even as an enemy attacks. **Miss:** you're caught in place while danger closes in |
| **Smuggler's Path** | You spend time looking for a secret way in or out of a place that might have one. Mark **exhaustion**, roll **Luck** | **Hit:** you find a hidden path (GM details it). **10+:** something valuable along it. **Miss:** you find it, but someone is using it right now |
| **Fleet of Foot and Hand** | always | **+1 Finesse** (up to +3). That makes Finesse **+3** |

*(Other options: Cross Country (extra exhaustion box, can mark injury instead), Don't Shoot the Messenger (Counterfeit and Luck for messenger tricks), Traveler Extraordinaire.)*

## Roguish feats (already marked)
**Acrobatics** (climb, vault, jump) and **Sneak** (get in or out unseen).

## Weapon skill (choose one)
**Quick Shot**: pairs with the shortbow. Roll **Luck** (close range). Hit: inflict injury. **10+** choose 2, **7-9** choose 1: don't mark wear / don't mark exhaustion / reposition (and maybe range) / keep the target at bay.
*(Alternatives: Trick Shot, Harry a Group, Disarm.)*

## Equipment (starting value 9; suggested spend: 9)

| Item | Wear | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Shortbow** (6) | 4 | Close range. **Quick Shot.** *Short Limbs:* mark wear to fire a quick shot at far range |
| **Leather Armor** (3) | 2 | *Flexible:* when grappling, mark exhaustion to ignore the foe's first choice |

Load: 2 of 4 before being burdened (max 8).

## Nature (choose one)
- **Competitive:** *Clear your exhaustion when you take dramatically unnecessary risks to show off.*
- **Dutiful:** Clear your exhaustion when you take on a dangerous or difficult task on behalf of another.

## Drives (choose two)
- **Wanderlust:** advance when you finish a journey to a clearing. *(You just finished one!)*
- **Crime:** advance when you illicitly score a significant prize or pull off an illegal caper against impressive odds.
- *(Also available: Discovery, Infamy.)*

## Connections (suggested)
- **Professional: Nick's Thief.** You tried to blaze a new trail between two clearings. When you share info after **Read a Tense Situation**, you both get +1. When you **help** them with a roguish feat, you get the 2-exhaustion help options for 1 exhaustion.
- **Friend: Noah's Tinker.** When you help them, mark **2 exhaustion for +2**.

## Your tie to Bertram's Cove (optional)
You have a sibling in the Cove, **Bill "Hookpaw" Hayward**, a hard-fisted squirrel you haven't spoken to in years. You're not sure whether you want to say hello or prove you've made it.

## Quick tips
- You're the **fastest** and **luckiest**. Use **Parkour** to get in, out, and across anything.
- **Swinging Ship to Ship** (a special rule in this adventure) is your spotlight (roll **Finesse**).
- Remember: at **7-9** you succeed with a cost. Don't fear it.
- You're not great at reading people or situations (Cunning -1). Ask a teammate.
