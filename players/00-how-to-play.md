# How to Play Root (Player Cheat Sheet)

You play a **vagabond**: a wanderer who doesn't fit in the clearings. You get into trouble, help (or hurt) the factions, and change the story. The GM plays the world and everyone else in it.

## The only dice rule

Roll **two six-sided dice and add one stat** (Charm, Cunning, Finesse, Luck, or Might).

| Total | Result |
|---|---|
| **10 or more** | You get what you wanted |
| **7 to 9** | You get it, **but** with a cost or complication |
| **6 or less** | It goes badly. The GM makes something happen |

You only roll when you do something risky or tricky that matches a **move**. If it's easy, the GM just says yes.

## Your five stats

| Stat | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Charm** | Persuading, reading people |
| **Cunning** | Tricking, noticing, planning, reading a dangerous scene |
| **Finesse** | Sneaking, climbing, picking locks, aiming |
| **Luck** | Risky gambles ("Trust Fate"), quick shots |
| **Might** | Fighting, smashing, protecting |

## What you can do (the basic moves, your "default options")

| I want to... | Move | Roll |
|---|---|---|
| Talk someone into something | **Persuade an NPC** | Charm |
| Learn what someone is really thinking | **Figure Someone Out** | Charm |
| Lie, bluff, or distract someone | **Trick an NPC** | Cunning |
| Size up a dangerous situation | **Read a Tense Situation** | Cunning |
| Sneak, climb, pick a lock, pick a pocket (a trained feat) | **Attempt a Roguish Feat** | Finesse |
| Do something risky with no other move | **Trust Fate** | Luck |
| Smash something | **Wreck Something** | Might |
| Help or hinder another player's roll | **Help or Interfere** | none (costs exhaustion) |
| Ask another player to follow my plan | **Plead with a PC** | none |
| Fight | **Engage in Melee**, **Grapple**, **Target** | Might / Might / Finesse |

You also have **your playbook moves** (on your character sheet) and **weapon skills**.

### Fighting in one minute
- **Engage in Melee** (roll + Might): on a hit, you and your foe **trade harm**.
  - **10+:** pick 3 of: **inflict extra harm** / **suffer less harm** / **shift range** / **frighten them**
  - **7-9:** pick 1
- **Armed** attacks cause **1 injury**. **Unarmed** cause **1 exhaustion**.
- Enemies have **morale**. Scaring them often ends a fight faster than hurting them.
- **Armor** can soak injury (mark **wear** on the armor instead).

## Your three "health" tracks

| Track | Meaning | When it fills up |
|---|---|---|
| **Injury** | Physical wounds | You're dying. Danger! |
| **Exhaustion** | Energy and will | You collapse |
| **Depletion** | Supplies and small gear | You're out of supplies |

- Special gear has **wear** boxes. When full, **it breaks**.
- **Clear exhaustion** by **doing your Nature** (it's on your sheet), by resting, or by being **pleaded with** and agreeing to someone's plan.

## Helping each other

- **Help or Interfere:** mark **exhaustion** to give **+1** to another player's roll (after they roll), or **-2** to hurt their roll (rare).
- **Plead with a PC:** once per session per player. If the other player agrees to your plan, they **clear 1 exhaustion**.
- **Connections:** each of you has two. They give **small bonuses** with specific friends (on your sheet).

## Reputation

You have a **reputation** with each faction: **Denizens, Marquisate, Woodland Alliance**. Doing things earns **prestige** (good) or **notoriety** (bad). More prestige improves your rep. High or low reputation unlocks special moves. Ask the GM before using one.

## Growing your vagabond

You have two **drives** on your sheet. When you do what a drive says, you **advance**: choose an upgrade (more stats, new moves, more skills, extra health, new connections). Once per drive per session.

## How to be a great player (no experience needed)

1. **Say what your character does**, not what you roll. "I try to talk the guard down" rather than "I roll Charm".
2. **Ask questions.** "What do I see?" "Who do I recognize?"
3. **Use your playbook moves.** That's what makes your character special.
4. **Help each other.** Mark exhaustion to help a friend's roll.
5. **Missing is fun.** A 6 or less makes the story more interesting.
6. **Be bold.** Vagabonds are brave, clever, or both.
7. **It's okay to say "I don't know what to do."** Ask the GM or the group.
8. **Speak up** if anything makes you uncomfortable. We can change it.

## Why are you in Bertram's Cove?

Pick one (or invent your own):
- **A job:** you've been hired for a delivery or a small rescue.
- **A rumor:** you've heard the Cove is full of opportunity (or treasure).
- **A person:** you're looking for, or avoiding, someone.
