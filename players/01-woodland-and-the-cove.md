# The Woodland and Bertram's Cove (Players' Briefing)

*No spoilers. This is what any vagabond would know.*

## The Woodland

A great forest crossed by winding paths and dotted with **clearings** where denizens live. The forest between clearings is dangerous: bandits, bears, harsh weather. Travel is slow.

The old **Eyrie Dynasties** (birds of prey) once ruled. They tore themselves apart in a civil war. In the power vacuum, the **Marquise de Cat** arrived from a foreign empire with soldiers and engineering. Now the **Eyrie** is back and at war with her, while a rebel network, the **Woodland Alliance**, tries to take the Woodland from both.

No faction is purely good or evil. Every faction helps some and hurts others.

## The factions you'll meet

| Faction | In a sentence | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Marquisate** | Foreign invaders or builders of order and industry, depending on who you ask | Disciplined cats in gleaming armor, forts, supply ships |
| **Woodland Alliance** | Rebels who want to take the Woodland back from both empires | Secret meetings, ragged fighters, rebel pirates |
| **Eyrie Dynasties** | The old rulers, now trying to retake the Woodland | Birds of prey. Mostly off-stage here |
| **Denizens** | Ordinary folk who just want to live | Fishermen, shopkeepers, kids |

## Vagabonds

You're not part of any faction. You live in the gaps: you take jobs, make friends and enemies, and slowly decide whose side you're on. Everyone you meet will treat you based on your **species**, your **reputation**, and how you act.

## Bertram's Cove (what you know on arrival)

- A busy **lake town** on the eastern shore of the **Grand Lake**, where the **Alberdon River** flows in.
- Under **Marquisate** control for years. It's a key **supply hub** and the lake is the town's lifeblood.
- A **rebel** captain, **Captain Sparrowhawk**, has been raiding Marquisate ships. Soldiers are hunting him. His face is on every post.
- The ship crossing from the western shore takes **a day and a night**. Going around by land would take **two weeks** through forest.
- The town's folk love the lake and have mixed feelings about the soldiers. The Marquisate keeps order and improved life, but demands obedience.

## Your ship

You arrive aboard the **Twice Sunk**, a battered ship that has been sunk and raised twice (that's the whole joke). You are the passengers and crew for this crossing. At dawn, you see the Cove.

## Tips for new players

- **Talk to people.** Everyone has opinions about the war.
- **Ask what you notice.** The GM will answer.
- **You can sneak, charm, fight, build, or run.** All of them work.
- **Not every problem needs a sword.**
