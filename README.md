# Root RPG - Bertram's Cove Project

AI assistants: read this file first and update it whenever decisions or deliverables change.

## Goal
First-time GM running Root: The Roleplaying Game (Magpie Games) for 6 friends who are new to RPGs. The adventure is based on the free module *Bertram's Cove* (note: "Cove", not "Grove").

## Constraints
- Single session of 2-3 hours; may continue into more sessions later.
- Keep it simple, exciting, and beginner friendly.
- GM owns the Root core rulebook but wants quick cheat sheets so they can avoid looking things up.
- Prefer Markdown (.md) output.

- Keep GM spoilers out of `players/` and `teasers/`.
- Mark anything we invented as **(added)** so it isn't confused with module content.

## Adventure at a glance (spoilers)
Working title (ours): **"The Night the Cove Nearly Burned"**.
- Setting: Bertram's Cove on the Grand Lake, occupied by the **Marquisate**; **Woodland Alliance** rebels raid under the legend **Captain Sparrowhawk**.
- Secrets: Sparrowhawk is a costume worn by fisherman **Kell Sweetgrass**. The real Alliance leader is town treasurer **Saffron Goodacre**, who plans to **bomb the hospital** (sick sailors, the Cuttlefish Cough) and ambush responders. The only volunteer is reckless young **Aiden Birch**, who may blow himself up by accident.
- Session spine (ours): dawn arrival -> burning supply ship -> free investigation (docks/Daisy, fort, town/rebels) -> dusk roundup -> night climax at the hospital cart -> aftermath and cliffhanger.
- Tools (ours): 4-box **clock** (Dusk, Nightfall, Midnight, Boom), a **three clue rule**, and added NPC **Tilda Fenn** (nervous rebel who can leak the plot).
- Side threads saved for later (from the module): **To Catch a Spy** (Michel Lambert), **Treasure in the Depths** (Wavebreaker gold, Daisy's dive rig), **Love and Rebellion** (Kell and Declan).

## Decisions made
- Use the playbooks the players chose; adapt the quickstart's pregen bonds into optional "ties" per player.
- Players arrive on the *Twice Sunk* (from the module).
- Burning-ship opener, three leads, and the cart showdown are our structure, not the module's.
- Teasers avoid the bomb, hospital, spy, Saffron and Kell secrets.
- The "prize" is knowledge (Saffron and Sparrowhawk's secrets) plus prestige, a purse (6-Value, added), and favors. The Sparrowhawk bounty is NOT paid for stopping the bomb.
